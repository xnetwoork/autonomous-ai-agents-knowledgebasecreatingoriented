# Autonomous AI Knowledgebase Milestones

A structured set of 50 milestones to build autonomous, knowledge-creating agents that continuously research, optimize, and expand project scope with scientific rigor and community authority.

## Milestones

1. **Vision & entropy target** — Define the mission and a measurable “high‑entropy creativity” target for novel, valuable outputs.
2. **Domain scope & boundaries** — Specify what the agents must cover, exclude, and prioritize across disciplines.
3. **Stakeholder & community map** — Identify authoritative scientific communities and public cohorts for validation.
4. **Ethical charter** — Establish safety, bias, and harm‑avoidance principles aligned to scientific norms.
5. **Governance model** — Define decision rights, escalation paths, and audit responsibilities.
6. **Agent role taxonomy** — Catalog roles (researcher, curator, evaluator, optimizer, historian).
7. **Process ontology** — Standardize tasks, workflows, and states for research and knowledge creation.
8. **Knowledge artifact types** — Define what the agents produce (claims, models, datasets, protocols).
9. **Data source registry** — Maintain a living index of trusted, relevant sources.
10. **Licensing & rights controls** — Ensure compliant usage and provenance of all sources.
11. **Acquisition pipelines** — Automate ingestion from journals, datasets, APIs, and community signals.
12. **Credibility scoring** — Score sources by authority, reproducibility, and peer review signals.
13. **Novelty discovery** — Continuously scan for under‑explored topics to drive inventive creativity.
14. **Change monitoring** — Detect updates, retractions, and shifts in consensus.
15. **Normalization & schema** — Convert data into a unified, queryable schema.
16. **Provenance graph** — Track lineage for every claim, dataset, and conclusion.
17. **Ground‑truth baselines** — Curate trusted benchmark datasets and reference models.
18. **Deduplication & quality filters** — Remove noise, redundancies, and low‑quality inputs.
19. **Multimodal ingestion** — Support text, code, figures, tables, and experimental data.
20. **Extraction pipelines** — Convert raw inputs into structured claims and entities.
21. **Hypothesis generator** — Produce diverse, high‑entropy hypotheses aligned to evidence gaps.
22. **Experiment templates** — Standardize methodologies for testing hypotheses.
23. **Autonomous literature review** — Continuously synthesize state‑of‑the‑art findings.
24. **Benchmark harvesting** — Track and ingest new evaluations and leaderboards.
25. **Evaluation metrics** — Define scientific validity, novelty, impact, and utility metrics.
26. **Retrieval & indexing** — Implement scalable search and retrieval for evidence grounding.
27. **Embedding governance** — Manage model versions and semantic drift checks.
28. **Memory hierarchy** — Implement short‑term, long‑term, and episodic knowledge stores.
29. **Chain‑of‑evidence** — Enforce traceable reasoning for every conclusion.
30. **Citation enforcement** — Require citations for all externally sourced claims.
31. **Conflict resolution** — Resolve competing claims with transparent arbitration.
32. **Temporal validity** — Track when knowledge becomes outdated or superseded.
33. **Confidence calibration** — Quantify uncertainty for each knowledge artifact.
34. **Human‑in‑the‑loop** — Integrate expert review for critical milestones and disputes.
35. **Automated peer review** — Simulate peer review criteria and checklist validations.
36. **Continuous learning policies** — Define what, when, and how to update the corpus.
37. **Self‑optimization loops** — Optimize data gathering and research strategies automatically.
38. **Resource budgeting** — Control compute, time, and cost for long‑horizon research.
39. **Security & access controls** — Protect sensitive inputs and internal models.
40. **Privacy & redaction** — Remove personal or restricted data from ingestion streams.
41. **Toolchain integration** — Connect simulators, solvers, labs, and external APIs.
42. **Sandboxed experimentation** — Test new methods without contaminating production knowledge.
43. **Failure analysis** — Run postmortems on incorrect or low‑value outputs.
44. **Reproducibility checks** — Verify results can be re‑created from inputs and methods.
45. **Cross‑domain transfer** — Apply discoveries from one domain to accelerate others.
46. **Community contribution pipeline** — Ingest verified public contributions at scale.
47. **External validation partnerships** — Collaborate with authoritative institutions.
48. **Publication cadence** — Release validated knowledge updates regularly.
49. **Impact measurement** — Track real‑world adoption and scientific influence.
50. **Strategy refresh** — Periodically re‑assess scope, priorities, and methods.
